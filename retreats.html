About Retreats

More Than a Weekend Away

A retreat here isn’t a holiday from your life — it’s an investment in how you meet it. We’ve built these days around one honest goal: giving your nervous system enough time and enough safety to genuinely settle, not just perform relaxation for forty-eight hours and snap back on the drive home.

That takes more than a nice view, so we’ve curated retreats combining horses, land, silence, stillness, movement, connection, good food, and experienced practitioners who know exactly what they’re doing. We’ve spent eleven years improving and regenerating this land, and it’s ready for you to enjoy.

For Teams

Corporate Retreats

Bring your team somewhere they can actually be a team, and uncover each other with a shared quiet, shared presence, and time with horses that read a room faster than any facilitator can. We customise half-day, full-day, and overnight retreats for groups of up to fourteen, built around your team’s real needs rather than a generic template. It’s a genuinely different kind of offsite — one that tends to the nervous system first, and lets the connection follow naturally from there.
